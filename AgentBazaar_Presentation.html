Motivation & Solution
Problem Statement & The AgentBazaar Approach
Eliminating manual P2P bargaining friction while preserving complete price confidentiality.
Challenges in Traditional Platforms (OLX / Quikr)
1. Uncomfortable Haggle & Lowball Spam

Buyers open with 40-50% unrealistically low offers, tiring out genuine sellers.

2. Slow Asynchronous Chat Replies

Back-and-forth messaging drags out over hours or days across dead threads.

3. Information Asymmetry & Regret

Revealing budget or urgency early weakens negotiation leverage.

4. Premature Phone & Location Exposure

Contact numbers are leaked before both parties are committed to a deal.

The AgentBazaar Solution
1. Dual Autonomous AI Agents

Buyer AI & Seller AI conduct structured multi-round negotiation in seconds over Socket.IO.

2. Strict Private Limit Isolation

Buyer ceiling (Pmax) and Seller floor (Pfloor) never cross prompt or API boundaries.

3. Game-Theoretic Math + 6-Layer Guardrails

Boulware/Linear/Conceder pacing, Tit-for-Tat reciprocity & hard limit clamping.

4. Dual Human Approval & Masked Handover

Phone & meetup location unlock only after both Buyer & Seller approve the agreed price.

Privacy Isolation Guarantees in Code

  • API Response Stripping: negotiationController masks counterparty private configs from both REST payloads and Socket.IO broadcasts.

Negotiation Core 4
Axelrod's Reciprocal Tit-for-Tat & Condition Elasticity
Responsive counter-moves that reward opponent good faith, resist stonewalling, and price item condition.

1. Reciprocal Tit-for-Tat Adaptation

IF ΔPopp > 0: Δrecip = round(ΔPopp × Mstyle × 0.5)
Price = clamp(Baseline ± Δrecip, [Min, Max])
  • Proportional Match Multiplier (Mstyle): FIRM: 0.70×, BALANCED: 1.00×, FLEXIBLE: 1.25×.
  • Concession Freeze Defense: If opponent makes ΔPopp = 0 concession in intermediate rounds, agent applies a freeze penalty (-10% buyer / -8% seller range).

2. Near-Gap Auto-Convergence Gate

  • When opponent's ask/offer is strictly within bounds AND (Offers Crossed || Final Round || Gap ≤ max(₹800, 3%)), the engine returns ACCEPT immediately.

3. Item Condition Discount Elasticity

Condition Factor Bargaining Effect
New0.80×Seller discount attenuated (0.85×); strong anchor defense
Like New0.90×High resale value retention; smaller counter steps
Good1.00×Standard market resale baseline
Fair1.15×Noticeable wear justifies firmer buyer discount
Used1.25×Heavy wear; higher discount flexibility expected

Once an agent hits its hard ceiling (Pmax) or floor (Pfloor), dialogue explicitly states the absolute limit instead of misleadingly repeating "raising/reducing offer".

The 6-Layer Programmatic Verification Pipeline

Layer 1: Action Whitelist

Allows only OFFER, ACCEPT, REJECT; substitutes rule-based move on invalid output.

Layer 2: Hard Limit Clamping

Buyer clamped ≤ Pmax; Seller clamped ≥ Pfloor; blocks out-of-bounds ACCEPT.

Layer 3: Monotonic Direction

Prevents backtracking: Buyer offers cannot decrease; Seller asks cannot increase.

Layer 4: Crossing Auto-Accept

Converts to ACCEPT whenever Buyer Offer ≥ Seller Ask within private bounds.

Layer 5: Turn & Round Policy

Turn 1 must be Buyer OFFER/REJECT; enforces currentRound ≤ maxRounds.

Layer 6: Limit Leak Redaction

Detects and redacts any message containing exact confidential ceiling or floor numbers.

Post-Negotiation Workflow
Dual Human Approval, Privacy Handover & Reputation
Human-in-the-loop verification from agreed proposal to offline handover and trust scoring.
STEP 1

Dual Human Approval Gate

PENDING_APPROVAL → CONFIRMED
  • Agents cannot auto-transfer funds or bind transactions silently.
  • Listing status automatically locks to RESERVED upon deal creation.
  • Both Buyer & Seller must explicitly click Approve within 48 hours.
  • Declining immediately releases product back to ACTIVE.
STEP 2

Privacy-Protected Handover

CONFIRMED → COMPLETED
  • Phone numbers & meetup location stay masked until dual confirmation.
  • Once CONFIRMED, contact info & time window unlock safely.
  • Users meet in person, inspect item, and pay via Cash / UPI directly.
  • Marking handover complete sets Deal COMPLETED & Product SOLD.
STEP 3

Reputation & Review Moderation

POST-COMPLETION TRUST
  • Only verified participants of a COMPLETED deal can submit 1–5 star ratings.
  • Dynamically recalculates averageRating & totalReviews on User profile.
  • Verified buyer reviews display cleanly on Seller info inside Product Details.
  • Users can Flag abusive or fake reviews for Admin resolution.
